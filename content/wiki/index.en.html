This Wiki explains why the project exists, the principles behind it, and how each tool is used.

What this is

The Decentralized Infrastructure of Right is a set of open-source protocols and tools. Its aim is to let the enforcement of a contract be chosen freely among many guarantors: stakes are held by a contract, disputes are decided by someone both sides chose beforehand, and evidence is kept by the parties themselves and can be checked by anyone.

What is authoritative

This Wiki is explanation. Authority rests in three places: the law texts and code in the repository, the bytes of the release, and the contract on chain. Where the two differ, these prevail; if you find a difference, please raise it in the repository.
