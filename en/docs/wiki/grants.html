Grants

A grant is a record in your ledger: who it is for, which work, for how long, with a terms file attached. Whoever receives it can verify it on their own.

Issuing a grant

  1. Open “Grant” and press “New grant”, or press “Grant to someone” on a record’s detail page.
  2. Fill in the grantee: the other side’s 0x… address. “Recent addresses” beside it lists addresses you have used.
  3. Press “Choose a record” and pick a work that is on chain.
  4. Drop in the terms file and choose the validity: 7, 30 or 90 days, or Custom.
  5. Press “Add to ledger”, press again on the confirmation card, then put it on chain as with a record.

What the terms say

The ledger records the address, the work, the period and the terms file’s fingerprint. Whether the grant is exclusive, where the work may be used, the price and what happens on breach all go in the terms file. When the grantee recomputes the terms file’s fingerprint and it matches the one in the grant, they know it is the very file attached at signing.

Sending it

On the grant’s detail page, pick one way to send it:

Grant file
Press “Export grant file” for a .zkgrant file. It carries your ledger, so the grantee can run the full check. This is the one to prefer.
Grant code
Press “Copy grant code” for a line of text starting with zikaron-grant:, easy to paste into a chat. It lets the grantee check the signature and the validity.

Revoking

Press “Revoke grant” at the bottom of the grant’s detail page, confirm, and put it on chain as well. A revocation is final, and every grant passed on from this one ends with it.

Receiving a grant

As a User, open “My grants”, press “Add grant”, paste the grant code or drop in the grant file, and press “Verify and add”. Each grant gets a card showing its verdict:

VerdictMeaning
All passedAll six checks passed; you can rely on it
Has gapsSome checks have not come through: confirmation on chain is pending, or the issuer’s ledger is missing (when you added a grant code alone). Check again later, or ask for the grant file
FailedA check found a problem
Revoked by issuerThe issuer has revoked the grant

The app re-verifies the whole collection on its own from time to time. When a grant is about to expire, gets revoked, or its issuer hands the ledger to a new key, the Alerts page tells you.

Credentials

To show a grant to your customers, open it in “My grants” and press “Create credential” for a line of text and a QR code. Anyone can paste the text into Verify › Verify grant to check it.

Sublicensing

A grant that passed all six checks has “Sublicense to others” on its detail page, for granting it on to someone else. The sublicense goes into your own ledger and ends when the upstream grant expires or is revoked, so set its validity within the upstream’s.
