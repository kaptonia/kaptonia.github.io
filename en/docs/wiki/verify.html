Verifying

When you receive a grant or a record kit, check it yourself under “Verify” in the sidebar. A check reads the public chain and the files you were given, is free, and is there for both Recorders and Users.

Verifying a grant

  1. Open Verify › Verify grant.
  2. Paste the grant code, or press “Choose a file” and pick the grant file you received.
  3. If you also received the work and the terms file, put them in “File” and “Terms”.
  4. Press “Verify”.

The app checks six things, each with a light:

Grant signature
Signed by the issuer’s key.
Issuer ledger intact
The issuer’s ledger links up entry by entry.
In the issuer’s ledger
The grant is an entry in that ledger.
Confirmed on chain
It is on chain.
Within validity
Now falls within the period the grant states.
Not revoked
Having read the issuer’s whole ledger, the grant still stands.

The result is one of three:

ResultWhat to do
All six checks passedYou can rely on it. If you put in the file and terms, see that they read “Match”
Has gapsSome checks are still waiting for an answer; the grey light gives the reason. Check again in a few minutes, or ask the other side for what is missing
FailedA problem was found; hold off and talk to the other side

A grant code alone allows checking the signature and the validity; for the full check, ask for the grant file.

Verifying a record kit

Open Verify › Verify record and drop the kit (a .zkit file or a folder) onto “Drop a record kit or record file”; the check starts as soon as it lands. The conclusion is one of two:

  • All match: the kit is whole, the signatures hold and every record is on chain. Which originals the kit carries, and whether they match, shows in the “Original in kit” column of “Record by record” below.
  • N mismatches: something does not line up; confirm with the other side before accepting it.

The “Record by record” table has one row per record, with whether it is on chain and when it first went there.

When the other side uses another chain

A record kit states which chain it is anchored on. When that chain is missing from your settings, the page says so; press “Add” and fill in the nodes in Settings › Network. A check reads your main network plus every chain under “Read-only networks”.

Looking up a ledger

Verify › Look up a ledger: fill in the “Author address” and press “Read” to see the records that address put on chain and the grants it issued.
