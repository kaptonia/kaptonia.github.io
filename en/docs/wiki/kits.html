Presenting records

When you need to prove something, pick the records that bear on it and export a record kit: a folder holding those records, the original files and the on-chain proofs. Whoever receives it can verify it on their own computer.

Exporting a record kit

  1. Press “Export record kit” on a record’s detail page; that record is already selected.
  2. To add more, press “Choose…” and pick the entries that bear on the matter; the rest stay with you.
  3. “Originals of the selected records” lists the files the records were made from. For any that is missing, drop the original into “Attachments”.
  4. Write a note and press “Verify and export”. The app checks the kit itself first and writes it once it passes, with “Record kit exported to …”.

A record kit is an ordinary folder: zip it and email it, copy it to a USB stick, or hand it to an arbiter. Before handing it over, glance at what the chosen entries say: record names and notes are inside.

Putting it online

You can also place a record kit at any https address for others to fetch. You do the upload; fill in the “Published address” in Settings › Network, press “Save published address”, then “Check publication”, and the app checks that the copy online matches the one here.

Early and steady records weigh more

In “Readings and reference (not in the package)” in the export page’s side column, press “Calculate” for three numbers:

First recorded
The earliest moment the content can be shown to have existed.
Max depth
How many on-chain records hold this content.
Continuity
How steadily it went on chain from the first record to the last.

A ledger that records the drafts, revisions and final version along the way convinces more than one with the final version alone.
